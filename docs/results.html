Cross-Study Meta-Analysis of Spaceflight Response

Across 13 NASA GeneLab spaceflight transcriptomics studies (22 ecotype/genotype comparisons; 534,547 gene comparisons), cross-study meta-analysis identified 2,550 consensus significant spaceflight DEGs (1,291 upregulated, 1,259 downregulated; FDR < 0.05 in both Fisher combined p-value and REML random-effects meta-analysis). Five of the 13 studies are continuous-dark BRIC experiments (OSD-37, 121, 147, 205, 321), and 2,429 of the 22,405 Fisher-significant entries are unmapped numeric microarray row identifiers; both are flagged for re-analysis in the manuscript.

AraGWAS Catalog Overlap & Trait Enrichment

Integrating 462 AraGWAS studies (1,523,262 Bonferroni-significant associations across 279 phenotypes) revealed that GWAS candidate genes are strongly enriched among spaceflight DEGs (763 overlap genes, OR = 4.23, p = 4.45e-102). 90 GWAS phenotypes were significantly enriched, led by climate adaptation and leaf ionome traits (molybdenum, cobalt, sulfur).

Machine Learning & Ecotype Prediction

A nested 5-fold cross-validated random forest recovered the consensus labels almost perfectly (AUC = 0.999, average precision = 0.986). Its features include the Fisher and meta-analysis statistics that define those labels, so this reflects recovery of the labelling rule rather than independent predictive performance. Direction concordance across studies (0.188) and Fisher combined p-value (0.149) were the top features.

Geographic Distribution & Latitudinal Gradients

Mapping 982 accessions from the 1001 Genomes Project demonstrated a significant latitudinal gradient (Spearman ρ = -0.125, p = 8.97e-5), while elevation showed a weak non-significant trend (ρ = -0.047). A regression on population group, latitude and elevation explained 34.9% of predicted score variance (R² = 0.349).

Chromosome 4 Locus

The 31 SNPs used for ecotype scoring are the 31 highest-scoring associations in the AraGWAS catalog, selected on GWAS score alone. Twenty-nine lie in a 14 kb block on chromosome 4 (positions 1,257,343–1,271,408; AT4G02820 to AT4G02860). In AraGWAS, all 29 positions are associated with three LC-MS leaf metabolite features (M216T665, M172T666, M130T666), not with ionome traits: none is shared with Co59, As75 or Mo98 (ion_content_locus_sharing.csv). The ecotype scores therefore rest largely on one metabolite-associated locus.

Statistical Power (Q4)

With only four ecotypes (n = 4) having both spaceflight transcriptomic data and geographic coordinates, correlations between elevation and transcriptomic responses are badly underpowered (power_analysis_results.csv).
